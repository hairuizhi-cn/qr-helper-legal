Privacy Policy

QR Code Scanner & Generator · Last updated: September 29, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to QR Code Scanner & Generator (the "App"). Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how the App handles information, uses device permissions, and protects your data. Please read it carefully before use.

We Do Not Collect Your Personal Information

The App is a local tool: QR content you generate, scan history, and style preferences are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and we have no access to your data.

The App has no account system. We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and we do not perform behavioral analytics or ad tracking.

All local data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the App.

Device Permissions

Camera: used only when you actively use the "Scan" feature to recognize QR codes in real time. It is never used in the background and does not take or store photos.

Photo library: used only when you tap "Save" to write a generated QR image to your photo library, or when you actively pick a logo/background image. Only the image you select is accessed.

Third-Party Services

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store / StoreKit). Payment information is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; the App never receives or stores your payment account details.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
